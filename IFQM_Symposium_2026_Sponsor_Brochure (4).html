About IFQM

The Indian Foundation for Quality Management (IFQM)

is a not-for-profit organisation committed to promoting Quality, Operational Excellence and Innovation across India.

For over three decades, IFQM has been a catalyst for organisational excellence—empowering people, enabling institutions and shaping a culture of continuous improvement and world-class performance.
